Questions

        
Good to know

      

      

        
Do I need to know anything about computers?
No. You tell us about your business and send photos from your phone. We handle the design, hosting and publishing.

        
How long does it take?
Most one-page sites are ready to preview within a week after we get your photos and information.

        
Who owns the website?
You do. Your domain is registered in your name, and if you ever leave, we hand over every file at no charge.

        
What does the care plan cover?
Hosting, security, and small updates like new prices, photos, hours and seasonal notices. Text us the change and it's on your site within two days.

        
Can my site be in another language?
Yes. We've built sites with English and Bangla, and we can add Spanish, Arabic or another language your customers speak.

        
Can you take online payments or bookings?
For most small businesses we start simple, with tap-to-call and text-message ordering. If you need payments or booking, we'll talk through options and costs first.
